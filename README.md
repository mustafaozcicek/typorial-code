Writing remains a fundamental technology for recording and sharing information. Today, written language also feeds the training of leading AI models. Questions of copyright, consent, and privacy invite designers to reconsider typographic conventions and ask who—or what—can read what we write.
Typorial is an experimental type design project that explores the boundary between human and machine reading. By rearranging Latin letters according to predetermined configurations, it creates the illusion of encrypted information. Once a human reader recognizes the typesetting pattern, familiar words emerge from the apparent disorder.
